About Me

Building Intelligent Systems

AI development, backend engineering, and automation focused on reliable products that solve practical business problems.

I’m an AI Developer at Kmphitech LLP in Surat, where I build the AI and backend layers of client products. Over the past 1.5 years, that has meant vision-LLM pipelines that read invoices, receipts, and passbooks; LLM generation workflows for meal, workout, and recipe apps; real-time German and English voice agents; and the FastAPI and Django services that tie them together.

I focus on what makes AI dependable in production: strict schemas instead of free-form text, deterministic rules before an LLM call, retries with model fallback, caching and Batch APIs to keep costs down, and test suites that catch regressions before users do.

I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science with Honors in Data Science, and came to AI engineering through internships in data science, machine learning, model fine-tuning, and Python backend development.
